About me

I recently completed my PhD in Chemical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, under the supervision of Prof. Ashok N. Bhaskarwar. My doctoral work combined mathematical modelling with experimental investigation of colloidal gas aphron generation across batch, continuous-flow, semi-batch, and recirculation modes.

I am interested in connecting measurable dispersion behaviour with the fluid-mechanical and interfacial processes that govern gas incorporation and stability.

Doctoral thesis
Mathematical Modeling of the Generation of Colloidal Gas Aphrons

Research

How do operating conditions and flow configuration influence the formation and evolution of a gas–liquid dispersion? My doctoral research approached this question through complementary models and measurements.

01 / Generation

Colloidal gas aphrons

Experimental investigation of surfactant-stabilized gas dispersions produced in rotating-disc generators, examining generation behaviour under different operating configurations.

02 / Dynamics

Mathematical modelling

Coupled nonlinear ordinary differential equations describing the evolution of gas and liquid in continuous-flow systems, with experimental and numerical validation.

03 / Stability

Recirculation and gas holdup

Investigation of batch and recirculation operation, assessing how recycling affects dispersion volume, gas holdup, and stability over time.
